# Design references (inspiration only)

Screenshots Sayandip shared on 24 Nov 2026 while planning the product. They show the level of motion and polish he wants. They are third-party designs: study the techniques; never copy their assets, code, layouts or text.

## inspiration/ (4.png - 9.png)
Screenshots of animated product websites seen in short social-media videos, for example a parrot brand site and Apple's AirPods Pro page with particles bursting around the product. Sayandip also described a laptop floating and opening and a soda can splitting apart as the kind of effect he loves.

## libraries/ (10.webp - 29.webp)
- **10-27:** the prompts.picsmob.com library (Solenne, Heatcheck, Lunare, Popwave, Afterglow, Rouvelle and more) and the scrolltide.co library: templates, components, its MCP and its pricing (Ora, NOPAL, MIRÉ, Scowlby, Pouterby, Toucan, Macaw, Solano, Carrara, Grana and Plum; components such as the Scroll Key glass keycap, Particle Globe, Carousel Ring, Image Tunnel, Wave Gallery, spiral/DNA and tilt carousels).
- **28-29:** the Scrolltide library grid (the Solano 3D store, Pouterby, MIRÉ, components, pricing).

## How to use them
- The techniques behind each effect, and where we use them, are in docs/design/MOTION-3D.md ("Techniques"). The decision to build everything in-house is ADR-0008.
- Phase 0 demo: show the hero side by side with 2-3 of these (Sayandip picks which). The bar: does ours feel as premium while fitting a careers product and meeting our performance budgets?
