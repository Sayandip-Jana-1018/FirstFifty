# Brand and naming

## Codename
"FirstFifty" (as in "be among the first fifty applicants") is the working name. It can become the brand if it passes the checks below.

## Naming workshop (Phase 0)
1. Generate about 10 candidates around speed, trust, light and clarity, including FirstFifty itself.
2. Screen each against the criteria; drop the failures.
3. Shortlist 3, each with a one-line story, a mock logo on glass and the check results.
4. Sayandip chooses; ADR; he buys the domain.

Criteria:
- Short (ideally 2 syllables, under 10 letters); easy to say and spell in Indian English, Hindi and Bengali.
- A usable domain (.com preferred; .in or .app acceptable) available at the normal registration price.
- No conflicting trademark in IP India's public register in classes 9 (software), 35 (recruitment and placement services) and 42 (software as a service).
- No confusing match on Google Play, the App Store, LinkedIn, X or Instagram.
- Honest: no promise of guaranteed jobs, nothing misleading.
- Works as an app icon and as a word in a sentence ("check it on X").

## Voice and tone
Honest, warm, sharp. Plain words, short sentences, Indian English. Confident, never hype. We say what we did, not what we wish: "Verified at the source", never "100% guaranteed". Celebrate users' wins and never shame their gaps ("3 skills to add", not "you're missing skills").

## Visual identity
- A glass monogram that refracts light; its motion is light sweeping across it.
- One brand hue (docs/design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md), dark-first.
- 3D glass objects instead of photos of people.
- Company logos only to identify an employer on a listing.

## Copy rules
- Every number on the site is live and true (jobs today, companies read), or it isn't shown.
- No fake urgency ("only 2 spots left"), no fake scarcity, no fake reviews or testimonials.
- Pricing pages show the full price, renewal terms and how to cancel.
