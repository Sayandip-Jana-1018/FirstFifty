# Vision

## One line
Make India's early-career job hunt honest and fast: every real tech job, verified, the moment it's posted; your best application for it in one tap; and later a direct line between verified students and the companies that want them.

## The problem
- **Fake and junk listings.** 93% of Indian job seekers have run into fake or suspicious job offers, and 51% can't tell a real recruiter from a scammer (Indeed survey, reported 2026; docs/research/SOURCES.md).
- **Speed decides.** Good fresher roles collect hundreds of applications in the first days; students hear about them late, through forwarded Telegram messages or stale aggregator copies.
- **Noise.** Big portals earn from employers, so consultancy reposts and spam sit next to real roles.
- **Unequal access.** Most product companies never visit tier-2 and tier-3 campuses; those students depend on off-campus discovery.
- **Generic resumes.** One resume sent everywhere fails screening software, and students don't know which keywords they're missing.

## Who it's for
1. **Students and freshers (primary):** final-year and recent CS/IT graduates in India (2025-2028 batches) looking for SDE, DevOps/SRE, data, AI/ML, QA and security roles.
2. **Early-career engineers (secondary):** 0-3 years, often at service companies, wanting a product-company switch.
3. **College placement cells (paying customer, from Phase 5):** want better off-campus placement numbers.
4. **Employers (later):** want verified, ranked candidates without the spam.

Personas:
- **Riya,** 2027 batch, tier-2 college in Punjab, wants her first SDE job. Checks Telegram groups at night; was once asked for a "training fee" by a fake recruiter.
- **Arjun,** 14 months at a service company in Pune, wants a Bengaluru product role. Applies late because he finds openings a week after they're posted.
- **Ms. Kaur,** placement officer at a private university: 3,000 students, 40 visiting companies, judged on placement percentage.

## The promise
- **Real:** only official sources, meaning the company's own hiring system or careers page, with a source badge on every job.
- **Fast:** new jobs appear within minutes of first showing up at the source; alerts follow within a minute.
- **Fit:** ranked for you, with a match score that explains itself.
- **Ready:** tracker, ATS check and a tailored resume that never invents facts.
- **Safe:** scam checks on any link or recruiter message; privacy by default.

## Why we win
- **Data quality is the moat.** Coverage, freshness and dedup across thousands of official sources is boring, hard and compounding. Most competitors buy stale scraped feeds.
- **Incentives.** We earn from job seekers and colleges, not from employers paying for volume, so quality is the business.
- **Design.** Daily-use screens in a polished Liquid Glass system, where most Indian job apps look dated.
- **Distribution.** Student communities and campus pilots, starting from Sayandip's own college.
- **Founder-market fit.** The founder is the user. He already runs a scanner that reads about 13,500 postings per run across 120+ official boards, an ATS resume builder, and shipped SplitIT's Liquid Glass UI.

## The landscape
| Player | What they do | The gap we fill |
|---|---|---|
| Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, foundit | Huge portals, employer-paid | Noise, consultancy reposts, slow for freshers, dated UX |
| Instahyre, Cutshort, Wellfound, Hirist | Curated or startup-focused | Partial coverage, not fresher-first |
| Internshala, Unstop | Internships and competitions; companies pay | Not a verified full-time feed |
| Telegram and WhatsApp job channels | Fast forwards | Unverified, scam-prone, no personalisation |
| Simplify, Jobright, Teal (US) | Job copilots at $30-40/month | US-focused; not Indian pricing or sources |
| Grapevine, OnlyFresherHire, OwnYourCareer | Small Indian players | Fragmented; none combines official sourcing, speed and design |

## North star metric
**Weekly matched applications:** how many times active users open the official application page of a job we matched them to, per week. It needs real jobs, good matching and real intent at once. Supporting metrics: freshness lag, coverage, activation, D7/D30 retention, alert click-through, Pro conversion.

## Principles
1. Trust beats growth. One fake job costs more than a thousand real ones earn.
2. Speed is a feature, and so is calm. Fast data, a quiet interface, cinematic only at chosen moments.
3. The user's facts are sacred. We never invent, inflate or auto-submit.
4. Build the boring parts best: ingestion, dedup, uptime.
5. Measure, then claim.

## What we won't do
Auto-apply bots, scraping sites that forbid it, republishing full job descriptions, selling user data, paid "placement guarantees", social feeds or chat for their own sake.

## Where this goes
Year 1: the best verified job feed and toolkit for Indian tech freshers. Year 2: colleges and employers on the same platform; outcome data (who got hired where) improving matching; expansion beyond tech roles.
